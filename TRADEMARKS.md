# Trademark policy

The name "aicm" and the aicm logo identify this project and its maintainer, Atakan Atamert. This page explains how you may use them. It is meant to be read by people, not lawyers.

## The license covers the code, not the name

The code is available under the [Sustainable Use License](LICENSE). That license lets you use and modify the code within its terms. It does not give you any right to use the name "aicm" or the logo.

## Forks must use a different name

If you publish a modified version, or distribute a fork, give it a different name and logo that are not confusingly similar to aicm. You may say that it is based on aicm, for example "Foo, a fork of aicm".

## What you may do without asking

- Say that your product, script, integration or guide works with aicm, for example "works with aicm" or "exports data to aicm".
- Refer to the project by name when you write about it, review it, teach it or link to it.
- Redistribute unmodified releases and images under the name aicm, as long as you make clear they come from this project.
- Use the name and logo on slides, blog posts and community pages that talk about the project.

## What you may not do

- Imply that the maintainer endorses, sponsors or supports your product, service or fork.
- Use "aicm", or something confusingly similar, as the name of your product, service, company, domain or package.
- Offer a hosted service or paid product under the aicm name.
- Change the logo or combine it with your own marks.

## Anything else

If you are not sure whether your use is fine, or you want to do something this page does not allow, ask by email at atknatmrt@gmail.com. Most reasonable requests get a yes.
